# Bench wiring — one BMI160, direct or through one TCA9548A

This is the first bring-up wiring for **one BMI160 only**. Use **one** of the two
configurations below, never both at the same time.

- Board: ESP32-S3 DevKitC-1 **N16R8**
- Logic and sensor supply: **3.3 V**
- I2C: **SDA = GPIO8**, **SCL = GPIO9**, **100 kHz** for this test
- USB-C cable from the ESP32 to the PC. The CH343/UART port is the serial
  console at **115200 8N1**. The native USB port also works when the firmware is
  built with `CDCOnBoot=cdc`; `flash.sh --flash` detects it and adds that option.

Printed sheets:

| File | Use |
|---|---|
| `direct_bmi160.pdf` | BMI160 wired directly to the ESP32 |
| `mux_tca9548a_bmi160_ch0.pdf` | TCA9548A on the ESP32 bus, BMI160 on channel 0 |
| `netlist.csv` | Machine-readable connection list for both options |

The drawings are generated by `generate_schematic.py`. Edit the net table in
that file, not the PDFs. Regenerate from the workspace root with:

```bash
pixi run schematic
```

Python dependencies (`schemdraw`, `matplotlib`, `Pillow`) live in the workspace
root `pixi.toml`; there is no local virtualenv.

## 1. Parts

| Qty | Part | Notes |
|---:|---|---|
| 1 | ESP32-S3 DevKitC-1 N16R8 | The board already on USB |
| 1 | GY-BMI160 breakout | Purple 18 × 13 mm board; onboard ME6211 3.3 V LDO; 3.9 kΩ pull-ups; two headers |
| 1 | TCA9548A breakout | Needed only for the mux test |
| 1 | Breadboard | One power rail pair is enough |
| — | Jumper wires | Female-female for breakout headers |
| 2 | 100 nF capacitors | One at each board's supply pin, close to the pin |
| 1 | 10 µF capacitor | Bulk on the 3.3 V rail |

No external I2C pull-ups are needed for one GY-BMI160 wired directly to the
ESP32: the board already has 3.9 kΩ pull-ups on SDA and SCL. Do **not** add
4.7 kΩ on top unless you measure the bus and find it missing pull-ups.

With the mux, those 3.9 kΩ pull-ups sit on channel 0, not on the ESP32 trunk.
Most TCA9548A breakouts already fit 10 kΩ trunk pull-ups; check your board. If
the trunk has none, add one 4.7 kΩ pair from ESP32 SDA/SCL to 3V3. One pull-up
pair per bus segment is enough at 100 kHz.

### GY-BMI160 headers

The board has two rows of pins, not one:

| Header | Pin 1 | Pin 2 | Pin 3 | Pin 4 | Pin 5 | Pin 6 | Pin 7 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **P2** (7 pins) | `VIN` | `3V3` | `GND` | `SCL` | `SDA` | `CS` | `SA0` |
| **P1** (5 pins) | `OCS` | `INT2` | `INT1` | `SCX` | `SDX` | — | — |

`3V3` is the **output** of the onboard LDO (the PDF calls it `VDD`), not a
supply input. `CS` is the SPI chip select / I2C mode select (`CSB` on the bare
chip). `SA0` is the address-select digital input (`SDO`/`SA0` on the bare

### GY-BMI160 supply — use VIN at 3.3 V

| Supply choice | Wire | Leave open | Use |
|---|---|---|---|
| **3.3 V into VIN (required here)** | ESP32 `3V3` → BMI `VIN` | BMI `3V3` | One 3.3 V rail for ESP32, mux and sensor |
| 5 V into VIN | **Do not use** | — | Puts 5 V on the onboard pull-ups and on SDA/SCL |

The board schematic shows four 3.9 kΩ resistors (R1–R4) tied to **VIN**, pulling
up SDA, SCL, `CS` and `SA0`. Feeding `VIN` from 5 V would therefore put 5 V on
the I2C lines, above the ESP32 and BMI160 3.3 V limits. Feed `VIN` from the
ESP32 `3V3` rail and leave the `3V3` pin open. The LDO then makes the sensor
rail at about 3.2–3.3 V, and every pull-up sits at 3.3 V.

Do **not** power the `3V3` pin instead: that back-feeds the LDO output and
leaves the four pull-ups connected to an unpowered `VIN`. The bus would have no
pull-ups.

`CS` is already pulled high by R3, so the board defaults to I2C mode. `SA0` is
already pulled high by R4, so the board defaults to address `0x69`; tie `SA0`
to GND for `0x68`. Tying `SA0` to GND draws about 0.85 mA through R4, which is
fine.

**Label map for this exact board:** `CS` = `CSB` (mode select), `SA0` =
`SDO`/`SA0` (address-select digital input, not a data line). It is a static
strap: tie it to GND or leave it high, do not toggle it while the bus is active.
`SCX`/`SDX` are the secondary interface, `OCS` is its OIS chip select, and
`INT1`/`INT2` are interrupts. All five are unused today and stay open.

## 2. Where the pins are on the ESP32-S3 DevKitC-1

Both headers are 22 pins. Pin 1 is at the top of the board, near the antenna;
the USB connectors are at the bottom. This is the official v1.1 header order:

```
J1 (left side, pin 1 at top)          J3 (right side, pin 1 at top)
 1  3V3                                1  GND
 2  3V3                                2  TX  / GPIO43
 3  RST                                3  RX  / GPIO44
 4  GPIO4                              4  GPIO1
 5  GPIO5                              5  GPIO2
 6  GPIO6                              6  GPIO42
 7  GPIO7                              7  GPIO41
 8  GPIO15                             8  GPIO40
 9  GPIO16                             9  GPIO39
10  GPIO17                            10  GPIO38
11  GPIO18                            11  GPIO37
12  GPIO8   <-- SDA                   12  GPIO36
13  GPIO3                             13  GPIO35
14  GPIO46                            14  GPIO0  (BOOT)
15  GPIO9   <-- SCL                   15  GPIO45
16  GPIO10                            16  GPIO48
17  GPIO11                            17  GPIO47
18  GPIO12                            18  GPIO21
19  GPIO13                            19  GPIO20
20  GPIO14                            20  GPIO19
21  5V                                21  GND
22  GND                               22  GND
```

Use **J1 pin 12 for SDA** and **J1 pin 15 for SCL**. Use any `GND` pin; J1 pin
22 and J3 pins 1, 21, 22 are all ground. Use **J1 pin 1 or 2 for 3V3**.

Some clones silk-screen the GPIO number without the `GPIO` prefix (`8`, `9`),
and some boards label the header pins only on the bottom. Check the board
silkscreen before trusting the physical position.

## 3. Option A — BMI160 directly on the ESP32

Use this first. It proves the sensor and the firmware before the mux adds a
second variable.

### ESP32-S3 DevKitC-1 → GY-BMI160

The 7-pin row is `VIN 3V3 GND SCL SDA CS SA0`; the 5-pin row is
`OCS INT2 INT1 SCX SDX`. Read the silkscreen; do not trust position alone.

| ESP32 pin | Header pin | Wire | GY-BMI160 pin | Note |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `3V3` | J1 pin 1 or 2 | red | `VIN` | 3.3 V into the LDO and the four pull-ups |
| `GND` | J1 pin 22 (or any GND) | black | `GND` | Common ground |
| `GPIO8` | J1 pin 12 | blue | `SDA` | I2C data |
| `GPIO9` | J1 pin 15 | green | `SCL` | I2C clock |
| `3V3` | red strap | — | `CS` | Tie **HIGH** to 3V3; R3 already does, but tie it explicitly |
| `GND` | black strap | — | `SA0` | Tie to **GND** → `0x68` |
| — | none | — | `3V3` | LDO output; leave open |
| — | none | — | `INT1` | Leave open |
| — | none | — | `INT2` | Leave open |
| — | none | — | `SCX` | Secondary interface; leave open |
| — | none | — | `SDX` | Secondary interface; leave open |
| — | none | — | `OCS` | Secondary OIS chip select; leave open |

`CS` is not driven by a GPIO in this test. It is a mode select, not a chip
select for I2C. A floating `CS` can put the BMI160 into SPI mode and make it
invisible to the I2C scan. `SA0` is the address strap: GND = `0x68`, 3V3 or
floating = `0x69` (R4 pulls it high). Use GND for the first test; the firmware
probes both anyway.

Do not connect the mux at the same time.

### Direct-sheet checklist

- [ ] ESP32 `3V3` → BMI `VIN`
- [ ] BMI `3V3` open
- [ ] ESP32 `GND` → BMI `GND`
- [ ] ESP32 `GPIO8` → BMI `SDA`
- [ ] ESP32 `GPIO9` → BMI `SCL`
- [ ] BMI `CS` → `3V3`
- [ ] BMI `SA0` → `GND` (0x68), not both rails
- [ ] `INT1`, `INT2`, `SCX`, `SDX`, `OCS` open
- [ ] 100 nF from BMI `VIN` to `GND`, close to the breakout
- [ ] 10 µF across the 3.3 V rail
- [ ] Continuity checks below pass before USB is plugged in

## 4. Option B — TCA9548A mux, BMI160 on channel 0

The mux is the **only** device on the ESP32 I2C bus. The BMI160 hangs off
channel 0. Power is not switched by the mux; the BMI `VIN` pin still comes from
the 3.3 V rail.

### ESP32-S3 DevKitC-1 → TCA9548A

| ESP32 pin | Header pin | Wire | TCA9548A pin | Note |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `3V3` | J1 pin 1 or 2 | red | `VIN` (or `VCC`) | 3.3 V |
| `GND` | J1 pin 22 (or any GND) | black | `GND` | Common ground |
| `GPIO8` | J1 pin 12 | blue | `SDA` | Trunk SDA |
| `GPIO9` | J1 pin 15 | green | `SCL` | Trunk SCL |
| — | red strap | — | `RESET` | Tie **HIGH to 3V3**; floating resets the mux |
| — | black strap | — | `A0` | Tie to **GND** → address 0x70 |
| — | black strap | — | `A1` | Tie to **GND** |
| — | black strap | — | `A2` | Tie to **GND** |

Some boards label the supply `VCC` instead of `VIN`. Some label the address
pins `A0/A1/A2`, others `ADDR0/ADDR1/ADDR2`. The function is the same.

### TCA9548A channel 0 → GY-BMI160

| TCA9548A pin | Wire | GY-BMI160 pin | Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| `SD0` (or `SDA0`) | blue | `SDA` | Channel 0 data |
| `SC0` (or `SCL0`) | green | `SCL` | Channel 0 clock |
| `3V3` rail | red | `VIN` | Direct from rail, not through the mux; `3V3` pin open |
| `GND` rail | black | `GND` | Common ground |
| — | red strap | `CS` | Tie **HIGH to 3V3** |
| — | black strap | `SA0` | Tie to **GND** → 0x68 |
| — | none | `3V3` | LDO output; leave open |
| — | none | `INT1` | Leave open |
| — | none | `INT2` | Leave open |
| — | none | `SCX` | Secondary interface; leave open |
| — | none | `SDX` | Secondary interface; leave open |
| — | none | `OCS` | Secondary OIS chip select; leave open |

Leave `SD1/SC1` through `SD7/SC7` completely open. Do not fit wires on unused
channels; a floating channel pair is harmless, but a miswired one is another
device on the selected bus.

### Mux-sheet checklist

- [ ] ESP32 `3V3` → mux `VIN`
- [ ] ESP32 `GND` → mux `GND`
- [ ] ESP32 `GPIO8` → mux `SDA`
- [ ] ESP32 `GPIO9` → mux `SCL`
- [ ] Mux `RESET` → `3V3`
- [ ] Mux `A0`, `A1`, `A2` → `GND`
- [ ] Mux `SD0` → BMI `SDA`
- [ ] Mux `SC0` → BMI `SCL`
- [ ] BMI `VIN` → `3V3` rail
- [ ] BMI `3V3` open
- [ ] BMI `GND` → `GND` rail
- [ ] BMI `CS` → `3V3`
- [ ] BMI `SA0` → `GND` (0x68), not both rails
- [ ] BMI `INT1`, `INT2`, `SCX`, `SDX`, `OCS` open
- [ ] `SD1`–`SD7`, `SC1`–`SC7` open
- [ ] 100 nF at mux `VIN`, 100 nF at BMI `VIN`, 10 µF bulk on the rail

## 5. Power-up order

1. Wire everything with **USB unplugged**.
2. Set the straps: `CS` high, `SA0` low (0x68), mux `RESET` high, mux
   `A0/A1/A2` low.
3. Measure resistance from `3V3` to `GND` with the power off. It should not be a
   dead short. A few hundred ohms or more is normal on a board with capacitors;
   a stable 0 Ω means stop and find the bridge.
4. Check continuity of every wire in the checklist. Wiggle each jumper while
   measuring; breadboard contacts are the most common intermittent fault.
5. Plug the USB-C cable into the ESP32. The board should enumerate as
   `/dev/cu.usbmodem*`.
6. Open the monitor **before** resetting the board if you want to see the boot
   banner. The firmware does not wait for the monitor.
7. Press `EN`/`RESET` once after the monitor is open.

Power down before changing any wire. Do not hot-plug the BMI160 or the mux.

## 6. Continuity checks (power off, USB unplugged)

With a multimeter in continuity mode. Remember that the GY-BMI160 has 3.9 kΩ
resistors to `VIN` on SDA, SCL, `CS` and `SA0`; those paths show about 3.9 kΩ,
not a beep.

| Probe A | Probe B | Expected |
|---|---|---|
| ESP32 `3V3` | BMI `VIN` | beep |
| ESP32 `GND` | BMI `GND` | beep |
| ESP32 `GPIO8` | BMI `SDA` | beep |
| ESP32 `GPIO9` | BMI `SCL` | beep |
| BMI `CS` | BMI `VIN` | about 3.9 kΩ (R3); beep only if you added a hard strap |
| BMI `SA0` | `GND` | beep on the chosen strap only |
| BMI `SA0` | BMI `VIN` | about 3.9 kΩ (R4); do not strap both rails |
| BMI `3V3` | `3V3` rail | **not a direct short**; this is the LDO output |
| ESP32 `3V3` | ESP32 `GND` | **no beep** |
| ESP32 `GPIO8` | ESP32 `GPIO9` | **no beep** |
| ESP32 `GPIO8` | `3V3` / `GND` | **no beep** |
| ESP32 `GPIO9` | `3V3` / `GND` | **no beep** |

For the mux configuration, repeat with:

| Probe A | Probe B | Expected |
|---|---|---|
| ESP32 `3V3` | mux `VIN` | beep |
| ESP32 `3V3` | mux `RESET` | beep |
| ESP32 `GPIO8` | mux `SDA` | beep |
| ESP32 `GPIO9` | mux `SCL` | beep |
| Mux `SD0` | BMI `SDA` | beep |
| Mux `SC0` | BMI `SCL` | beep |
| Mux `A0`, `A1`, `A2` | `GND` | beep each |
| Mux `SD0` | Mux `SC0` | **no beep** |
| Mux `SD0` | `3V3` / `GND` | **no beep** |
| Mux `SC0` | `3V3` / `GND` | **no beep** |

## 7. Expected I2C addresses

| Device | Address | Condition |
|---|---|---|
| TCA9548A | `0x70` | `A0 = A1 = A2 = GND` |
| BMI160 | `0x68` | `SA0` tied to GND |
| BMI160 | `0x69` | `SA0` floating or tied to 3.3 V (R4 pulls high) |

With the mux configuration, the upstream scan should show `0x70`. When channel 0
is selected, the downstream BMI160 also answers at `0x68` or `0x69`. The mux
itself still answers at `0x70` while a channel is selected; the firmware filters
that address out of the per-channel scan.

## 8. Flash and open the monitor

From the workspace root (`/Users/rifusaki/localRepos/uni`):

```bash
./invexp/esp320/scripts/flash.sh --flash
```

Or from `invexp/esp320`:

```bash
./scripts/flash.sh --flash            # compile + upload + monitor
./scripts/flash.sh                    # monitor only; no flash write
./scripts/flash.sh --flash --port /dev/cu.usbmodem5CBC3855071
```

If the upload cannot open the port, use the manual bootloader sequence:

1. Hold `BOOT` (GPIO0).
2. Tap `EN`/`RESET` once while still holding `BOOT`.
3. Release `BOOT`.
4. Rerun the flash command.

To open only the monitor:

```bash
./scripts/monitor.sh --port /dev/cu.usbmodem5CBC3855071
```

`monitor.sh` detects the port type. On the CH343 it opens with DTR and RTS
deasserted; asserting both holds the ESP32-S3 in reset through the auto-reset
circuit, which shows up as garbage bytes instead of firmware output. On native
USB it opens plain 115200 and expects firmware built with `CDCOnBoot=cdc`. Tap
`RESET` after the monitor opens to capture the boot banner. Commands are
line-buffered; press Enter after each one.

The scripts use FQBN `esp32:esp32:esp32s3` with
`FlashSize=16M,PSRAM=opi,PartitionScheme=app3M_fat9M_16MB`. Override with the
`FQBN` and `BOARD_OPTIONS` environment variables if your board differs.

## 9. Serial commands

Commands are single letters and case-insensitive.

| Key | Action |
|---|---|
| `s` | Scan the upstream bus (ESP32 side) |
| `m` | Scan all eight mux channels, one line per channel |
| `d` | Read the BMI160 directly on GPIO8/GPIO9 |
| `0` | Read the BMI160 through mux channel 0 |
| `r` | Stream accel and gyro at 10 Hz |
| `x` | Stop streaming |
| `i` | Print detected configuration, addresses and health |
| `?` | Print the command help |

### First run, direct configuration

1. `s` → expect `0x68` or `0x69`, no `0x70`.
2. `d` → expect chip ID `0xD1` and a first sample.
3. `r` → expect `[data] accel_g ... | gyro_dps ...` lines every 100 ms.
4. `x` → stop.

### First run, mux configuration

1. `s` → expect `0x70` only.
2. `m` → expect `CH0: 0x68 (BMI160)` or `CH0: 0x69 (BMI160)`; all other
   channels `none`.
3. `0` → expect chip ID `0xD1` and a first sample from channel 0.
4. `r` → stream from channel 0.
5. `x` → stop.

A healthy stationary sample should show roughly `accel z ≈ +1.000 g` (or
`-1.000 g` depending on mounting orientation) and gyro near `0 dps`. Small
offsets are normal; a gyro that sits at several hundred dps is not.

## 10. Troubleshooting

| Symptom | Likely cause | Check |
|---|---|---|
| `s` shows nothing | No power, no ground, or wrong pins | Continuity table; 3.3 V at BMI `VIN` |
| `s` shows nothing, mux config | `RESET` floating or low | Strap mux `RESET` to 3V3 |
| `s` shows `0x70` but `m` shows all `none` | BMI not powered, `CS` low/floating, wrong channel | BMI `VIN`, `CS` high, `SD0`/`SC0` |
| BMI answers at `0x68` but `d` fails init | `CS` in SPI mode or bad `SA0` strap | `CS` high; `SA0` only one rail |
| BMI answers at `0x69` instead of `0x68` | `SA0` floating; R4 pulls it high | Tie `SA0` to GND |
| `m` shows a device on every channel | Mux channel select not working, or `A0/A1/A2` wrong | Address must be `0x70`; check straps |
| Address is `0x71`–`0x77` | `A0/A1/A2` not all low | Strap all three to GND |
| Upload cannot open port | CH343 driver, cable, or boot mode | Data cable; manual BOOT/RESET sequence |
| Port disappears after reset | CH343 re-enumeration | Replug USB, reopen monitor |
| Samples freeze | I2C timeout or power droop | Shorter wires, 100 nF at sensor, check 3.3 V |
| Gyro drifts a lot | Normal bias or saturation | Keep still; check range and mounting |

## 11. What this test does not cover

The microSD card is not part of this wiring. USB serial is the logger for today.
Once the card arrives, the same bus can be exercised at 400 kHz and the sample
rate raised; the 100 kHz setting is deliberate for first contact.

For five sensors, keep one mux channel per BMI160 and give each sensor its own
`CS`/`SA0` straps. The mux switches SDA/SCL only, so each sensor still needs
`VIN` and `GND` from the rail. Do not parallel two sensors on the same channel
unless their addresses differ.
